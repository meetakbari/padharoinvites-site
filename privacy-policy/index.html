Who we are

Padharo Invites (“Padharo”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile app and website for sending event invitations and managing events such as weddings, engagements, baby showers and birthdays. Padharo is a product of KwikReach Solutions, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380015, India.

This policy explains what personal data we collect through the Padharo app, the website at padharoinvites.in, and the WhatsApp messages sent through Padharo, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

Hosts and guests

Padharo has two kinds of people:

  • Hosts are people who create an account and plan an event, along with the family members they invite to help (Admins, Helpers and Viewers).
  • Guests are people a host adds to an event so they can receive invites, reminders and event updates. Guests do not need to install the app.

For guest details, the host decides whom to invite and what to record. We process guest details on the host’s behalf, only to run that host’s event. Hosts must add only people they personally know and who would expect to hear from them about the event.

Information we collect

From hosts

  • Account details: name, mobile number and, if you give it, email address.
  • Event details: event type, functions, dates, times, venues, invitation card images or PDFs, and settings.
  • Planning records you enter: rooms, food choices, budget, expenses, payments between family members, gifts, notes and work areas.
  • Purchase records: which plan or message pack was bought for an event. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we do not receive your card or bank details.

About guests (entered by the host)

  • Name, mobile number (and a second number if added), family or household members, relation or side (“guest of”), and counts per function.
  • RSVP replies, food choices, room assignments, arrival status, gifts and notes the host records.

When guests reply on WhatsApp

  • The guest’s WhatsApp number and profile name, their reply (for example “Coming” or “Not coming”), and message status such as sent, delivered and read, as provided by the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Phone contacts

If a host chooses to add guests from their phone contacts, the app asks for permission first. Only the contacts the host picks are uploaded. We do not upload the whole address book.

Technical data

  • Device type, operating system, app version, crash reports and basic usage events, used to keep the app working and improve it.
  • This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, including verifying your mobile number with a one-time code.
  • To send invitations, RSVP requests, reminders, room details and event updates to guests on WhatsApp, as the host instructs.
  • To show hosts RSVP replies, counts, delivery and read status, and other event information.
  • To let family members a host invites see and edit the parts of the event their role allows.
  • To process purchases and show purchase history.
  • To give support, prevent misuse and spam, and meet legal duties.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use guest details for advertising or to message guests about anything other than the host’s event.

WhatsApp messages

Padharo sends messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When a message is sent, the recipient’s number and the message content pass through Meta’s systems under Meta’s own terms and privacy policy.

  • Messages are only about the host’s event: invitations, RSVP requests, reminders, room details and updates.
  • Every bulk message names the host, so guests know who invited them.
  • Opting out: a guest can reply “STOP” or use the opt-out option in a message at any time. We then stop all further messages to that number for that event, and record the opt-out.
  • A host may also send invites one by one from their own WhatsApp. Those messages go from the host’s phone, and we do not read them.

Who we share information with

We share personal data only as needed to run Padharo:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc., to deliver WhatsApp messages.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers, to store data securely.
  • Google Play and Apple App Store, to process in-app purchases.
  • Crash reporting and SMS one-time code providers, to keep the app reliable and secure.
  • Other members of the same event, as allowed by the roles the event Owner sets. For example, money details can be hidden from Helpers.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Our providers may process data outside India. When they do, we require them to protect it as this policy describes.

How long we keep information

  • Event data is kept while the event is active and for 12 months after the last function, so hosts can look back at it. Hosts can delete an event sooner.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • When an event or account is deleted, we remove its data from our live systems within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.
  • We may keep limited records longer where the law requires it, such as purchase and tax records, or records of opt-outs so we keep honouring them.

How we protect information

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access inside an event follows the roles the Owner sets, and access by our staff is limited to support and security needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell affected users and authorities about a serious breach as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Hosts can edit or delete most data directly in the app.

  • Guests: to stop messages, reply “STOP”. To have your details removed from an event, ask the host, or contact us with your mobile number and the event name.
  • Deleting your account: see our data deletion instructions.

We reply to requests within 7 days and complete them within 30 days.

Children

Hosts must be 18 or older. Hosts may record children as members of a guest family (for example, for headcounts or kids meals). We do not knowingly let children create accounts, and we do not use children’s data for tracking or advertising.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Padharo grows. We will post the new version here with a new date, and tell hosts in the app about important changes.

Contact and Grievance Officer

For any privacy question or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 working days.
