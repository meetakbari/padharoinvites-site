What Padharo does

Padharo helps you plan events and invite guests. It lets you build a guest list, send invitations and reminders on WhatsApp, collect RSVPs, and manage functions, rooms, food, money, gifts and your planning team. Some features depend on your event type and plan.

The Android and iOS apps are launching soon. Features may change as we improve the service.
