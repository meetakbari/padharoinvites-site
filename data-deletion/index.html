Delete your data

How to delete your Padharo Invites account and data, or remove your details from an event if you are a guest.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Padharo Invites app and sign in.
  2. Go to the More tab, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, then confirm with the one-time code sent to your mobile number.

If you are the Owner of an event that other family members still use, the app first asks you to hand ownership to someone else or delete that event.

Ask us to delete it

If you cannot use the app, send a deletion request to us by email or WhatsApp. Please send it from, or include, the mobile number on your account so we can verify it is you.

  • Email: kwikreach.solutions@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”
  • WhatsApp: +91 83472 00125

We confirm your request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days.

Delete one event

An event Owner can delete a single event from More, then Event settings, then Delete event. This removes the event’s guest list, RSVPs, rooms, food, money records, cards and messages history for everyone in that event.

If you are a guest

You may have received an invite because a host added your number to their event.

  • To stop messages: reply “STOP” to the message, or use the opt-out option in it.
  • To remove your details: ask the host to remove you from their guest list, or contact us with your mobile number and the event or host name, and we will remove your details from that event.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details: name, mobile number and email.
  • Events you own, with their guest lists, RSVPs, cards, rooms, food plans, money records, gifts and notes.
  • Your membership in events owned by others. Changes you made there stay in that event’s history under “Former member”.

Data is removed from live systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. We keep only what the law requires, such as purchase and tax records, and a record of opt-outs so we keep honouring them.

Signed in with Facebook or Meta?

If you ever connected Padharo using a Meta or Facebook login, you can also remove Padharo from Facebook: go to Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Apps and websites, select Padharo Invites and tap Remove. Then send us a deletion request as above to delete the data we hold.
